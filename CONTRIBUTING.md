# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in `go-semver`.

This project is **open source** (licensed under [Apache-2.0](./LICENSE)) so that
anyone can read, use, and build on the code. However, it is developed and
maintained solely by Snyk for Snyk's own needs, and **we do not accept external
contributions**.

That means:

- We are **not accepting pull requests.** PRs opened against this repository
  will be closed without review.
- We are **not accepting feature requests** or general issue reports.

You are still welcome to:

- **Use the code** under the terms of the [Apache-2.0 License](./LICENSE).
- **Fork the repository** and maintain your own modifications, as permitted by
  the license.
